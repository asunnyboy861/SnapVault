Privacy Policy

SnapVault — Last updated: May 7, 2026

🔒 SnapVault processes everything on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers.

Data We Collect

SnapVault does not collect any personal data. Specifically:

  • No personal information is collected
  • No usage analytics are gathered
  • No device identifiers are transmitted
  • No location data is accessed
  • No browsing history is tracked

How Your Data Is Processed

All data processing in SnapVault happens 100% on your device:

  • Screenshot Scanning: Photos Framework accesses your screenshots locally for categorization
  • AI Classification: Vision Framework performs OCR and classification entirely on-device
  • Search Index: All search data is stored locally using SwiftData
  • Notifications: Local notifications only, no push notification servers

Photo Library Access

SnapVault requests access to your photo library to:

  • Scan and categorize screenshots
  • Display screenshot previews
  • Delete screenshots when you choose to

Photo access is governed by iOS system permissions. You can revoke access at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos.

Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device using SwiftData. No data is ever uploaded to any server. If you delete the app, all associated data is permanently removed.

Third-Party Services

SnapVault does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services. The only external service used is Apple's StoreKit for In-App Purchases, which is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

Children's Privacy

SnapVault does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Since we collect no data at all, our app is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
