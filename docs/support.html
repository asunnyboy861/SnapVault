Frequently Asked Questions

How does SnapVault categorize my screenshots?

SnapVault uses Apple's Vision Framework to analyze your screenshots on-device. It reads text (OCR), detects QR codes, and classifies screenshots into 15 categories like OTP Codes, Shopping, Receipts, and more — all without sending any data to the cloud.

Is my data safe?

Absolutely. All processing happens 100% on your device. No screenshots, text, or metadata ever leaves your iPhone. We don't have servers, so there's nothing to hack.

Why does SnapVault need photo access?

SnapVault needs photo library access to scan your screenshots, display previews, and delete screenshots when you choose to. This access is controlled by iOS system permissions and can be revoked at any time.

What's the difference between Free and Pro?

Free includes scanning, categorization, OCR search, and temporary screenshot detection. Pro ($4.99 one-time) adds swipe-to-clean, batch delete, lock protection, advanced search filters, Spotlight integration, and data export.

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Go to Settings > Pro Features > Restore Purchases. Your purchase is tied to your Apple ID and will be restored automatically.

What are "temporary" screenshots?

Screenshots containing OTP codes, verification numbers, or QR codes are automatically detected and marked as temporary. SnapVault will remind you when they expire so you can clean them up.

Can I undo a deletion?

Deleted screenshots are moved to the Recently Deleted album in your Photos app, where they stay for 30 days before permanent deletion. You can recover them from there.

Troubleshooting

  • No screenshots found: Make sure you've granted Photos access in Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos
  • Categories seem wrong: Tap "Rescan All Screenshots" in Settings to re-classify
  • Notifications not working: Check Settings > Notifications > SnapVault
  • Pro not activating: Try "Restore Purchases" in Settings
